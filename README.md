Tales of Argosa Adventure Framework 68 - Caverns of Shennog

A long lost shrine to Shennog, goddess of night, madness, and mystery, has been rediscovered in a cave system in the southern Wistwood. What ancient relics might be waiting for those intrepid enough to venture within? 

As always, Adventure Frameworks are just that - adventure sites and scenarios with enough meat to run a session or three, but room for the GM to adapt and improvise to player choices. They tend to be low to moderate magic adventures. Over the last few years they've grown larger and generally speaking now include: rumours, travel events table, custom trinkets/loot, dungeon events table, a new magical item, at least one new monster, a map(s), primary dungeon/scenario details, and pregens. Written for the Tales of Argosa RPG, but easily adapted to any d20 based fantasy rpg.
